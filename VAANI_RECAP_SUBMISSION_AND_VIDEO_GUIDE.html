1. Executive Summary & The Problem

Over 63 million Indian micro, small, and medium enterprises (SMBs) — ranging from local dental clinics and boutique coaching institutes to real estate brokers, auto dealerships, and digital agencies — struggle with lead outreach. Hiring and training telecalling teams is prohibitively expensive, manual dialing is slow, language barriers across regional dialects hinder conversions, and valuable lead context is permanently lost once a phone call concludes.

2. The Solution: Vaani Recap

1. Autonomous Outbound Dialing with Caller ID

Owners upload prospect lists via Excel/CSV. The AI agent initiates outbound calls through the owner's verified forwarding caller ID, ensuring trust and direct callbacks. Conversations are spoken naturally across 10 Indian language modes using Gnani Timbre v3.0.

2. Real-Time STT & Lead Extraction

Calls are transcribed verbatim using Gnani Prisma v3.0 (handling Indian code-mixed accents). Transcripts are parsed into structured lead cards: Intent, Need, Budget, Objections, Follow-up Date, and verbatim Key Quote.

3. Spoken Evening Brief (< 60 Words)

Owners don't read dense spreadsheets after a long day. At close of business, the agent synthesizes a concise, audio-spoken evening recap in the owner's preferred regional voice persona, detailing won deals and urgent callbacks.

4. Zero-Redundancy Credit Caching

Every synthesized audio chunk and transcript is SHA-256 hashed and cached in local SQLite, ensuring zero duplicate API billing while syncing live state to Firebase Realtime Database.

3. Can You Use an AI Voice for the Video Demo?

YES, absolutely! Using a high-quality AI voiceover is standard practice and well-received in technical hackathons, especially for voice technology challenges. Here are the 3 easiest ways to record with an AI voice:

Method 1: Windows Clipchamp (Built-in & 100% Free)

1. Open the Clipchamp app from your Windows Start menu.
2. Click "Record & Create" → "Screen", and record your browser at localhost:3000 while clicking through the pages.
3. Click "Record & Create" → "Text to speech".
4. Paste each section's script from the table above, select voice (e.g., English (India) - Neerja or Prabhat), and drag the audio track onto your timeline!
5. Export as 1080p MP4.

Method 2: ElevenLabs / CapCut (Studio Quality)

1. Go to elevenlabs.io or open CapCut Desktop.
2. Paste the spoken script into Text-to-Speech using an energetic Indian/Global English voice.
3. Download the MP3 audio file.
4. Screen-record your app actions using Windows Game Bar (Win + Alt + R).
5. Lay the voiceover track over your video in any free editor (CapCut, Canva, Clipchamp).
